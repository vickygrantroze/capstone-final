Empowering Indigenous Entrepreneurs To Level Up Their Business!

Located in Durham Region | Serving the Greater Toronto Area

The T-Shirt Factory helps Indigenous entrepreneurs and artists build new skills, grow their inventory, and create new income opportunities through the art of screen printing.

  • Learn a new skill — Design and screenprint your own products with hands-on guidance.
  • Create new products — Customize t-shirts, hoodies, tote bags, jackets, pillowcases, canvas and more.
  • Grow your inventory — Add unique products to your existing business or launch something new.
  • Collaborate & connect — Organizations can partner with Indigenous artists from our roster on custom-branded, promotional or fundraising projects.
  • All supplies are provided — so you can learn, create and start screenprinting right away.
Whether you're an emerging or established Indigenous artist or an organization looking to collaborate, The T-Shirt Factory provides the tools, materials and opportunity to turn creative ideas into products.
