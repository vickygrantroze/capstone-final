OPEN HOUSE WORKSHOPS

EVERYONE IS WELCOME!

COST: $100

We’re inviting everyone to learn the art of screenprinting! By joining a workshop at the Ishkode T-shirt Factory you’re helping the community! 100% of the proceeds from every workshop go back to the community via local community outreach initiatives! ALL SUPPLIES INCLUDED!

What To Expect:

  • Beginner-friendly / no prior experience required workshop
  • 3 hour hands-on lesson
  • Learn from a trained professional screen printing artist.
  • Bring premade logos, logo ideas, or create a new design in class from scratch!

Take Home:

  • One T-shirt in your size, to be printed in class with your own design
  • One tote bag, to be printed in class with your own design
  • One framed screen, complete with your own design, ready to bring home and start creating countless merch for your business, events, or just for fun!

CLICK HERE If you’re a teacher, educator, school principal, or administrator - WE COME TO YOU!

An Indigenous artist will come to your school or classroom to guide students in creating a unique, meaningful, student-led school design. Students work together with the artist to develop their own design and learn the art of screenprinting.

Each student will have the opportunity to screenprint their design onto a t-shirt to take home!

The school retains ownership of the original framed screen, along with a starter basket containing everything you need to continue screenprinting.

Keep creating for years to come with your own student-led, Indigenous-created design!

A great opportunity for school fundraising! Parents, grandparents, and community members will love the opportunity to purchase and proudly wear a piece of their students’ artwork.
